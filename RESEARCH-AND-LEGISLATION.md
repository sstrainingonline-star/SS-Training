# Source review — 29 October 2026

The course is written for barbering and training premises in England. HSE sources generally address Great Britain; the fire-safety references below concern England and Wales. Scotland and Northern Ireland have different frameworks in some areas. Workplace-specific advice and the current official source take precedence over this introductory course.

## Teaching course

- [DfE, Keeping children safe in education](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/keeping-children-safe-in-education--2): the source currently identifies the September 2026 edition as in force for schools and colleges. Other providers should identify which duties apply to their provision. The module does not replace staff safeguarding induction or reading the required guidance.
- [NSPCC, recognising and responding to abuse](https://learning.nspcc.org.uk/child-abuse-and-neglect/recognising-and-responding-to-abuse): listening, factual recording and escalation. Follow current local procedures; do not submit real disclosures as learning evidence.
- [Equality Act / education provider duties](https://www.gov.uk/rights-disabled-person/education-rights): disability discrimination and reasonable adjustments.
- [EEF, SEND teaching approaches](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/guidance-reports/supporting-high-quality-teaching-for-pupils-with-send): explicit instruction, scaffolding and adaptation.
- [EEF, feedback](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/guidance-reports/feedback) and [metacognition](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/guidance-reports/metacognition): clear learning goals, actionable feedback and learner self-checks.

The barbering activities and scenarios are original applications. EEF research is largely school-based; the course does not promise identical outcomes or a guaranteed speed of progress for adult learners.

## Review before delivery

Add your local safeguarding contacts, incident route, first-aid and fire arrangements through your normal induction. Review source guidance when procedures or legal requirements change. A reference to an Act's original year does not mean subsequent amendments are excluded. Do not assume that every older regulation is obsolete simply because newer guidance is available.

The course distinguishes law, recommended safe practice and original teaching examples. It makes no claim of HSE, DfE, EEF, VTCT or other external approval. Written tasks are completion checks; word count alone does not assess the quality of reasoning.
